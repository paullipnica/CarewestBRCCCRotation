Program Overview

Bridgeland Riverside Continuing Care Centre (BRCC) will provide a continuum of specialized mental health, behavioural support, restorative, and continuing care services across four levels. The design allows residents to receive the right level of support based on their needs, while creating opportunities for stabilization, rehabilitation, independence, and, where appropriate, transition to more independence-focused environments.

2nd Floor (Secure) – Complex Mental Health

45 beds
Secure Environment Complex Mental Health 45 Beds

The 2nd Floor is dedicated to the Complex Mental Health (CMH) Program, a secure continuing care environment for adults with complex mental health, addiction, cognitive, behavioural, and neurological conditions that cannot be effectively supported in traditional continuing care settings.

Working from a person-centred and harm reduction philosophy, the program focuses on building stability, supporting meaningful daily living, promoting independence, and enhancing quality of life. Residents benefit from specialized mental health support, interdisciplinary care, recreation programming, physician and psychiatric involvement, and 24/7 security.

Residents who currently live at Rouleau will transfer to this floor if they require a secure environment.

3rd Floor (Secure) – Enhanced Behavioural Support & Mental Health Restorative Care

45 secured beds · New programming
Secure Environment 45 Beds New Programming

Important features of Level 3 include:

  • Significantly higher staffing ratios compared to CMH, including floor staff and allied health.
  • More dedicated leadership supports on days and evenings.
  • Improved environmental features designed to reduce harm.

30 Beds – Enhanced Behavioural Support Unit (EBSU)

Long-Term Care 30 Beds

The Enhanced Behavioural Support Unit provides highly specialized care for individuals whose behavioural, cognitive, developmental, or neurodiverse needs exceed the supports available in other continuing care settings.

Residents may require frequent 1:1 interventions and may present with persistent and repetitive behaviours. They may require intensive behavioural interventions, enhanced supervision, structured therapeutic approaches, and specialized clinical support. Staff work closely with interdisciplinary teams to promote safety, reduce distress, and improve quality of life.

15 Beds – Mental Health Restorative Unit

Short Stay Post-Acute Care 15 Beds

The Mental Health Restorative Unit is a short-stay stabilization and rehabilitation program for individuals transitioning from acute care or other settings who require further mental health assessment, treatment, psychiatric support, and skill development before moving to a longer-term community placement.

The focus is on recovery, rehabilitation, behavioural stabilization, and preparing residents for their next stage of care. Clients may transition to one of the floors at BRCC following stabilization.

4th & 5th Floors (Non-Secure) – Complex Mental Health

54 beds + 54 beds
Non-Secure Environment Complex Mental Health 108 Beds Total

The 4th and 5th floors are non-secure Complex Mental Health neighbourhoods serving residents whose needs are best supported in an open environment. Residents may be living with mental illness, addictions, cognitive impairment, acquired brain injury, or behavioural challenges, while benefiting from a greater degree of independence and community integration.

The floors support residents who are stable enough for a non-secure setting while still requiring specialized mental health services and interdisciplinary care.
