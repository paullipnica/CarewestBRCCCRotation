Decision Criteria

Bridgeland Rotation Selection Process

← Rotation Finder

To ensure the Bridgeland rotation selection process is fair, transparent and consistently applied, the following process and decision criteria will be used.

1. Advance Review of Rotations

Rouleau Manor staff will be provided with the available Bridgeland rotations one week prior to the selection process, allowing employees time to review the rotations and identify their preferred choices.

2. On-Site HR Support

Human Resources will be present on-site during the selection process to capture and document employee rotation preferences and support the administration of the process.

3. Rotation Selection Criteria

All eligible employees will submit their rotation preferences. Where more than one employee selects the same rotation, priority will be determined using the following criteria, in order:

1
Carewest Tenure

The employee with the greater length of service with Carewest will receive priority.

2
Rouleau Manor Tenure

If Carewest tenure is the same, the employee with the greater length of service at Rouleau Manor will receive priority.

3
Alphabetical Order

If both Carewest tenure and Rouleau Manor tenure are the same, priority will be determined by last name in ascending alphabetical order (A–Z).

How it works: Employees submit their preferred rotation. The tenure and alphabetical criteria are only used when two or more employees select the same rotation.
